=== FILES CHANGED ===

src/app/beranda/page.tsx
  CHANGE 1:
    Before: "Total Omzet"  (month summary card label)
    After:  "Uang Masuk Bulan Ini"
    Reason: AGENTS.md microcopy standard — avoid "Omzet", use "Uang Masuk".
            "Total Omzet" is accounting jargon; "Uang Masuk Bulan Ini" is
            plain language the UMKM owner understands immediately.

src/app/catat/pengeluaran/page.tsx
  CHANGE 1:
    Before: "Nama Pengeluaran"  (field label in Detail Pengeluaran section)
    After:  "Nama Biaya"
    Reason: "Pengeluaran" as a field label is redundant (the section is already
            titled "Detail Pengeluaran"). "Nama Biaya" is shorter, clearer, and
            matches the domain vocabulary used in project_brief.md ("nama item",
            "biaya usaha"). Avoids repeating the section title in the label.

src/app/pengaturan/page.tsx
  CHANGE 1:
    Before: title="Pengaturan Usaha"  (AppShell title prop)
    After:  title="Profil Usaha Kamu"
    Reason: AGENTS.md microcopy standard — "Pengaturan Usaha" → "Profil Usaha Kamu".
            The h1 inside the page already said "Profil Usaha Kamu" correctly;
            the AppShell title (shown in mobile header) was inconsistent.

  CHANGE 2:
    Before: placeholder="Nama usaha Anda"  (business_name input)
    After:  placeholder="Nama usaha kamu"
    Reason: AGENTS.md tone — app uses "kamu" (informal, warm) throughout.
            "Anda" is formal and inconsistent with the rest of the UI copy.
